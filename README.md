## What a "flights and hotels" search usually turns into

Run the search intent through a filter and three kinds of people fall out.

The first wants a package — flight plus hotel bundled, one payment, ideally one confirmation number. The second is comparison shopping, checking whether Agoda beats Booking.com on the same four nights. The third already knows the flight number and just needs a room near the airport.

All three run into the same wall. Online travel agencies price inventory differently, and no single OTA is consistently cheapest. Smaller properties often appear on only one or two platforms. Bundle pricing behaves differently again, because the discount is applied to the package, not the individual components.

So the practical question stops being "which site is best" and becomes "how do I see all of them at once without losing an afternoon."

## The price gap is the real story, not the cashback

Cashback is the part that gets advertised. The price spread is the part that actually moves money.

ShopBack's own write-up of Travel Planner cites hotel price spreads of roughly USD 50 to 200 per night for the same room across different OTAs. On a five-night stay, that's the difference between a mid-range trip and a nicer one.

A 90-day experiment published on ShopBack's Singapore blog logged six real bookings and found per-booking savings between SGD 80 and SGD 280 — SGD 340 total — with one activity booking where booking direct was actually cheaper. Take that as one editor's log rather than a promise, but the shape of it matches what anyone who price-compares hotels already suspects: the lowest option rotates by week and by property type.

Cashback rates vary just as much. The same hotel can carry 8% at one OTA and 5.5% at another on identical dates. Per-result cashback display matters here, because the cheapest sticker price isn't automatically the best effective price once the rebate is counted.

## The booking rules that decide whether you get paid

This is where most travel cashback quietly dies. The mechanics are boring and completely unforgiving.

**Activate first, always.** Cashback tracks through the referral created when you click from ShopBack to the merchant. Apply a coupon or start the booking before activating and there's no referral for the cashback to attach to.

**Only use codes featured on ShopBack.** A promo code from a coupon site, a browser extension, or a marketing email can be treated as an outside referral and void the cashback. A $20 outside code that kills a $90 cashback return is a bad trade.

**Finish in one session.** Opening a new tab or app, clicking a retargeting ad, letting the session expire, or bouncing to another site and back can all break the chain.

**Kill the extensions.** Coupon browser extensions and ad blockers can inject their own referral or strip tracking. Same for VPNs. Turn them off for the booking.

**Accept cookies on the merchant's site.** Several merchants can only confirm a transaction to ShopBack if you accept them.

**Don't book in the merchant's app unless you started there.** Booking inside the OTA app directly, without routing through ShopBack, won't track.

**Don't change the booking afterwards.** Cancelled, refunded, exchanged, or no-show bookings lose their cashback, and changes that generate a new booking ID are ineligible. If you might move your dates, price the flexibility before you count the rebate.

## How long the money takes

Travel cashback runs on a slower clock than retail, and that catches people out because the number looks big in the app while it's still pending.

- **Tracked:** appears as pending shortly after booking, typically within 48 hours.
- **Hotels:** confirmed around 30 to 60 days after checkout.
- **Flights:** confirmed roughly 60 to 90 days after travel.
- **Activities and day tours:** confirmed about 15 to 30 days after the activity date.

A January booking for a July trip pays out in the autumn. Budget accordingly if you're counting on the return for anything specific. ShopBack also expires cashback balances if there's no valid purchase on the account within 12 months, so a dormant account with money sitting in it is worth a look.

## When not to route through cashback

An honest comparison has to include the cases where it doesn't work.

**Award redemptions.** Spending airline miles or credit card points on award tickets doesn't earn ShopBack cashback. Those bookings sit outside the affiliate layer entirely.

**Elite status nights.** Some hotel loyalty programmes only credit elite-qualifying nights for bookings made directly with the brand. If you need the night for status, book direct and skip the rebate.

**Niche properties.** Small guesthouses, family-run pensions, and specialised villa rentals sometimes appear on only one OTA — and not necessarily one that's integrated. The comparison can't surface inventory it doesn't have.

**Complex multi-city itineraries.** Dedicated flight tools handle unusual routings and carrier combinations better than OTA-based search. Use them to find the fare, then check what cashback sits on the OTA that sells it.

**Bookings you're likely to change.** If a non-refundable rate and a refundable rate differ by more than the cashback is worth, take the flexibility.

There's a second honest caveat: cashback is a rebate, not a pricing edge. If an OTA is $200 more expensive, a 6% rebate doesn't rescue that booking. Compare the effective price — base price minus cashback — and let the arithmetic decide.

## Questions that come up a lot

**Is the price different if I book through ShopBack?**
No. The price you see is the OTA's normal price. Cashback comes out of the OTA's affiliate commission budget, not from a markup.

**Does cashback stack with credit card points?**
Yes. ShopBack pays at the affiliate layer, your card rewards come from the issuer. A hotel booked through a participating OTA on a miles-earning card can earn both.

**Do I need a subscription to use Travel Planner?**
No. The comparison is free, and rates are shown per result without a paid tier.

**Which OTA is cheapest?**
It flips. Some OTAs trend lower on certain property types and regions, others on different ones. The comparison exists precisely because there's no permanent winner.

**Do prices hold between comparison and checkout?**
Not always. OTA prices can shift within hours. If you see a price you want, open it immediately and complete the booking in the same session.

**Why is my cashback still pending?**
That's normal for travel. Pending is the protection against paying out on cancelled bookings. Confirmation follows the timeline on the merchant's page.

**What if the cashback doesn't show up?**
File a missing cashback claim with your booking confirmation, typically after the 48-hour window has passed with no pending entry.
